Switch C

Turns the falling piece into balls (or back into a block). You get 1 charge back every 5 lines.

Mode

Controls

  • ←→ Move
  • ↑ / Space Rotate (Z rotates back)
  • ↓ Soft drop
  • Shift / Enter Hard drop
  • C Switch block ⇄ ball
  • P / Esc Pause

On touch screens: tap to rotate, drag to move, drag down to soft drop, flick down to hard drop, flick up to switch.
