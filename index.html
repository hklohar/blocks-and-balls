Controls

  • ←→ Move
  • ↑ / Space Rotate (Z rotates back)
  • ↓ Soft drop
  • Shift / Enter Hard drop
  • C Switch block ⇄ ball
  • P / Esc Pause

On touch screens: tap to rotate, drag to move, drag down to soft drop, flick down to hard drop, flick up to switch.

How to play

  • ←↑↓→ / WASD Steer
  • Space / Enter Spin the wheel
  • P / Esc Pause

Animals grow the snake. Each rope spins the wheel (×1 to ×10), and every 3 ropes speeds you up. After 6 ropes, creatures appear by the ropes. Don't hit the walls or yourself!
