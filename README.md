# Blocks & Balls Arcade

Two small browser games in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step or dependencies. Use the switcher at the top of each page to move between them.

**Play:** https://hklohar.github.io/blocks-and-balls/

## Blocks & Balls (`index.html`)
A Tetris-like game where some pieces are glossy balls that roll off ledges and settle like marbles.
- Modes: Classic, Balls, Mixed (~30% balls), Alternating (waves of 4)
- Switch (C) turns the falling piece between block and ball form (limited charges)
- Keyboard: ←/→ move, ↑/Space rotate, ↓ soft drop, Shift/Enter hard drop, P pause
- Touch: on-screen buttons plus tap/drag/flick gestures

## Snack and Letters (`snack.html`)
A snake game: eat cute animals that carry letters to spell the word shown, and collect ropes.
- Every rope pauses the game and brings up a bonus wheel numbered 1 to 10. Spin it (tap Spin, or Space/Enter; it spins by itself after 2 s). The number it lands on multiplies that rope's points, then play resumes after a 3-2-1 countdown.
- After 3 ropes or at level 5, collectible creatures start appearing next to ropes. Catch them for bonus points and to fill your Collection tray; your best collection is saved.
- The creatures (Zappip, Embrix, Shellow, Budlet, Wispo, Pebbit, Frostail, Glimmoth) are original designs drawn with simple canvas shapes.
- Keyboard: arrows or WASD steer, P pause. Touch: swipe on the board or use the arrow buttons.
